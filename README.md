# MICROVERSE

A meditative microscopic strategy game. Draw living membranes, nudge poison away from food, protect the mother, and evolve a colony through **three dishes and thirty connected encounters**.

On macOS, double-click [Launch.command](prototype/Launch.command), or open [project.godot](prototype/project.godot) in Godot 4.7 and press **F5**. The project directory retains its original `prototype` name; the default now launches the complete journey.

- **Stillwater → Glassgarden → Blue Deep:** each dish has its own layout, current, objectives, and boss. Feed, rescue young spores, defend nurseries, separate toxins, and prepare charged attacks.
- **A competing ecology:** food thieves, cell-blocking parasites, dividing hunters, and fading rescue spores create overlapping decisions. Capture linked shield organisms to choose when a boss opens.
- **A different route each journey:** eighteen seeded missions surround twelve authored teaching and boss encounters. Objectives, creature combinations, arrival directions, ecological events, and visual recipes vary. The journey targets 30–40 minutes; completion time depends on play style.
- **Twelve mutations, six choices per colony:** build around precise small meals, large captures, filtering, predator feeding, or regrowing organelles. Evolve at authored milestones; repair or travel between generated missions.
- **Three starting colonies:** the Wanderer is available immediately; clearing Stillwater unlocks the Weaver, and clearing Glassgarden unlocks the Symbiont.
- **Persistent progress:** automatic saves, exact mid-encounter resume, encounter retries that retain earlier evolution, and a species journal that survives new journeys.
- **Living microscopy:** animated translucent organisms, drifting pond life, fused absorption, visible organelle damage, and bundled soothing ElevenLabs music and effects. No network connection or API key is needed to play.
- **Ecological events:** harvest a drifting bloom before its grazer, gather a sleeping garden before cysts hatch at marked sites, and watch a harmless giant pass below the dish.
- **Choose your habitat:** after the opening lessons, take food-rich water with more hunters or a sheltered route with smaller blooms and a guaranteed rare visitor. The choice survives saves and retries.
- **A living home pond:** name actual rescued organisms, watch them grow and visit companions, and earn three small pond gardens through optional feats. The final victory brings your evolved Mother and rescued life into one quiet scene.
- **A growing field journal:** 29 entries, including twelve creature forms that appear gradually through the dishes, a young Bell bud, and three rare helpers: Moonlace, Glass Harp, and Glowdrift. New organisms rise into focus and unfurl; the live guidance names approaching danger and its deadline.

Keep the mother outside your capture loops. A loop enclosing her or cutting through her body shows a warning and cannot capture anything; rejecting it costs no health, food, or cell recovery. Free connections elsewhere remain available.

Choose **New journey** after relaunching to play the longer route. Existing saves retain their original twelve-encounter journey. New saves preserve the complete generated route, including retries. The game remains designed for deliberate drawing below the user's four-segment-per-second maximum.

Three complete scripted journeys passed without retries: **34.17 minutes** at three segments/second with 1.5 seconds of deliberation, and **44.33–48.46 minutes** at two segments/second with 2.5 seconds of deliberation. All use imperfect aim and the new mother exclusion rule. Menus are excluded; these are pacing/feasibility samples, not human win-rate or enjoyment measurements.
