# MICROVERSE

A meditative microscopic strategy game. Draw living membranes, nudge poison away from food, protect the mother, and evolve a colony through **three dishes and fifteen connected encounters**.

On macOS, double-click [Launch.command](prototype/Launch.command), or open [project.godot](prototype/project.godot) in Godot 4.7 and press **F5**. The project directory retains its original `prototype` name; the default now launches the complete journey.

- **Stillwater → Glassgarden → Blue Deep:** each dish has its own layout, current, objectives, and boss. Feed, rescue young spores, defend nurseries, separate toxins, and prepare charged attacks.
- **A competing ecology:** food thieves, cell-blocking parasites, dividing hunters, and fading rescue spores create overlapping decisions. Capture linked shield organisms to choose when a boss opens.
- **A different route each journey:** three seeded special visits punctuate twelve authored teaching and boss encounters. Objectives, creature combinations, arrival directions, ecological events, and visual recipes vary.
- **Twelve mutations, six choices per colony:** build around precise small meals, large captures, filtering, predator feeding, or regrowing organelles. Evolve at authored milestones; repair or travel between generated missions.
- **Three starting colonies:** the Wanderer is available immediately; clearing Stillwater unlocks the Weaver, and clearing Glassgarden unlocks the Symbiont.
- **Persistent progress:** automatic saves, exact mid-encounter resume, encounter retries that retain earlier evolution, and a species journal that survives new journeys.
- **Living microscopy:** animated translucent organisms, drifting pond life, fused absorption, visible organelle damage, and bundled soothing ElevenLabs music and effects. No network connection or API key is needed to play.
- **Ecological events:** harvest a drifting bloom before its grazer, gather a sleeping garden before cysts hatch at marked sites, and watch a harmless giant pass below the dish.
- **Choose your habitat:** after the opening lessons, take food-rich water with more hunters or a sheltered route with smaller blooms and a guaranteed rare visitor. The choice survives saves and retries.
- **A living home pond:** name actual rescued organisms, watch them grow and visit companions, and earn three small pond gardens through optional feats. The final victory brings your evolved Mother and rescued life into one quiet scene.
- **Narrated creature introductions:** all 44 discoverable creatures and forms have a cinematic close-up demonstrating their actual behavior, with bundled ElevenLabs narration and subtitles. First sightings offer a nonblocking **Watch film** choice; **Later** saves every new film in the Field Journal. Films can be skipped with Escape, paused with Space, or replayed from the journal. Choices are remembered across journeys.

Keep the mother outside your capture loops. A loop enclosing her or cutting through her body shows a warning and cannot capture anything; rejecting it costs no health, food, or cell recovery. Free connections elsewhere remain available.

Choose **New journey** after relaunching to play the focused fifteen-encounter route. Existing saves retain their original twelve- or thirty-encounter journey. New saves preserve the complete generated route, including retries. The game remains designed for deliberate drawing below the user's four-segment-per-second maximum.

Earlier thirty-encounter scripted journeys passed without retries: **34.17 minutes** at three segments/second with 1.5 seconds of deliberation, and **44.33–48.46 minutes** at two segments/second with 2.5 seconds of deliberation. These historical samples predate the focused route and long-link strain. Menus are excluded; scripted pacing does not establish human enjoyment.

The [focused-journey simulation report](prototype/artifacts/player-experience-simulation-2026-10-10.md) records Guard and Feeder results, long-link recovery, and graded captures. Later generated feeding visits require two hunter captures as well as food, and highly evolved colonies attract faster hunters. In the original thirty-encounter journey, full food stores draw faster Blue Deep hunters, alternating late visits end with a warned hunter wave, Guards rest for two encounters after blocking, and repair requires two wounded cores and an intervening encounter. Swift goal completion draws a larger wave. The [first](prototype/artifacts/challenge-pressure-2026-10-10.md) and [later-route](prototype/artifacts/challenge-pressure-followup-2026-10-10.md) saved-encounter reports record the balance checks. Human playtesting is still needed for feel and balance.
